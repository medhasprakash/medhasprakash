### 🔬 Research Focus
- **Quantum Chemistry**: Electronic structure theory (DFT, TD-DFT)
- **Intermolecular Interactions**: Non-covalent interactions in carbon nanostructures (cation-$\pi$ interactions in graphynes, $\pi$-$\pi$ interactions in cyclocarbon dimers, etc.)
- **Analytic Potential Modelling**: Parameterizing analytical potential models (LJ, ILJ, etc.) using `scipy.optimize`
- **Excited-State Modeling**: Time-Dependent DFT (TD-DFT) studies on the excited-state energy landscapes and photophysics of merocyanine molecules.
  
### 🛠️ Computational Toolkit

- **Quantum Chemistry Suites**: Gaussian, ORCA
